Our Story

From A Single Room
To A Movement.

FITRIX started in 2012 with a 400-square-foot room, four barbells, and a founder who refused to believe that world-class training was only for the wealthy. Emeka Mensah Sr. opened the doors with one rule: everyone who walks in here gets treated like a champion.

Twelve years later, FITRIX spans 18,000 square feet across two floors, employs 18 certified coaches, and has helped over 8,000 people transform their bodies and their mindsets. The room got bigger. The rule never changed.

We're not a franchise. We're not a chain. We're one gym, obsessed with doing one thing better than anyone else — getting you results.

Our Mission

Make Elite Training
Accessible To All.

We exist to close the gap between world-class fitness coaching and everyday people. No matter your starting point — your weight, your age, your experience level — FITRIX meets you exactly where you are and builds you up from there.

Our Vision

A Healthier City,
One Member At A Time.

We envision Osogbo — and eventually a continent — where fitness is a daily habit, not a luxury; Where communities are built around health, discipline, and the shared pursuit of becoming better. FITRIX is the starting line for that future.
